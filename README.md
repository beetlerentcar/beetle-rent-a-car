## Documents you may need

Requirements can vary by nationality, licence type and car category, so confirm with our team before booking.

- **UAE residents:** valid Emirates ID and a valid UAE driving licence (a passport copy may be requested)
- **Tourists and visitors:** valid passport, visit visa or entry stamp, and a valid driving licence from your home country (an International Driving Permit may be required)
